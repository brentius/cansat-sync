# Navigation Monte Carlo tables (SYNTHETIC)

Horizontal error is against simulated truth, over frames above the map floor and at least 2 s after the first fix; 160 descents per scenario. "≤10 m" is the fraction of descents whose worst such error met T1. NEES expectations are 2 and 16 (the 16 excludes the time-offset state, and is inflated by errors the filter absorbs into its bias states, so position NEES is the meaningful consistency figure). "recovered" is the fraction of descents in which the filter widened its prior at least once. "Δt err" is the camera time-offset estimate error. "search 99 %" is the 99th percentile over frames of the largest good-landmark prediction residual on the ground: what the matcher's search window must cover.
